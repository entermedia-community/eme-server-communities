Questions

Common questions

What does eME Salesbot cost?

$20 per month for each website. eME Small Business, the app your employees use for follow up, is included.

What can my customers do in the chat?

Ask sales questions, get support, schedule a meeting and place orders from your product menu.

Does eME Salesbot replace my staff?

No. It handles first contact and passes real work to your people in eME Small Business. The bot answers and collects, your people decide.

What is an eME?

eME is short for electronic me. Like email, but for you. Each employee trains their own eME, like eMike for Mike, to carry routine work with them in control.

Are there limits on AI usage?

No. eME runs on open models with flat pricing and no caps on tokens.

What happens to my business information?

It stays yours. eME is open source and self hosted, with no data harvesting and no vendor lock in.
