1. Demystifying the "Electronic Me"

Your EME is a personal digital twin

At the heart of the system is the EME (Electronic Me) persona. It acts as an AI assistant to your team members, keeping humans in control while augmenting their capabilities.

Each person trains their EME persona as a personalized digital twin that matches their communication style, schedule, and specialized knowledge, relying on human guidance for key actions.

People Driven: Digital Twin

Users stay in full control of their EME. By adding reference files, team guidelines, and personal preferences, leaders can adjust their EME for specific needs.

  • Clear and PreciseSet up for accurate administrative reports, legal reviews, or technical logs.
  • Friendly and HelpfulSet up for community outreach, student tutoring, or customer support.

Omnichannel Communication

My EME makes communication easier by connecting app users with team members who use email, helping everyone stay updated and productive in any project.

2. A Universal Productivity Booster for Everyone

Scenario A: Peer-to-Peer Logistical Help

In a regular chat between friends, like Christopher and Tammy, they talk about attending a weekend event. When they express interest, EME Christopher helps right inside the conversation.

The EME assistant checks their calendars, finds open times, confirms event details, and offers to handle tickets and travel plans. This keeps the plan simple without leaving the chat app.

3. Custom EME Servers

Build a server for your team

To support team projects, EME lets users build custom EME Servers. Built on a 100% open-source setup, software developers, local IT teams, and community organizers can quickly build custom servers and connect them to EME World to solve specific team needs.

You can reach all server features through simple chat screens supported by interactive forms, clean data tables, and helpful graphics.

EME World helps public groups and non-profits work faster using a single, secure digital system. By keeping humans in full control and letting each team member train their own EME persona as a digital twin, it boosts productivity and standardizes key actions while giving organizations complete oversight of their technology.
