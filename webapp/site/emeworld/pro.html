Questions

Common questions

What does eME Pro cost?

$1500 to install, then $150 per month hosting.

How is eME Pro different from eME Salesbot?

eME Salesbot is a ready made chat for your website with eME Small Business for your employees. eME Pro builds your own AI server and employee app around the way your business works.

Can I start with eME Salesbot and move to eME Pro later?

Yes. Start with eME Salesbot at $20 per month for each website, and move to eME Pro when you outgrow the standard app.

Does eME Pro replace my staff?

No. eME Pro augments the people you have. Each person trains their own eME to carry routine work, and people make the key decisions.

Are there limits on AI usage?

No. eME Pro runs on open models with flat pricing and no caps on tokens.

Who owns what we build?

Your knowledge stays yours. eME Pro is a 100% open source stack, with no data harvesting and no vendor lock in.
