It All Began With A Dream...

Crystal had recently broke the 5 minute mile & become a Cross-Country National Champion when she got hit by a car that ran a red light. Since she could no longer be a competitive runner, she went to study abroad in Uganda and then went on to live in Uganda where she spent 8 years building a school for orphan & needy children, a hotel and a safari company. After leaving Uganda and an abusive relationship, Crystal started all over again. She started, Courageous Speakers, a program teaching people how to be inspirational speakers & use their stories to get investors, funders, and clients. During this time she also traveled Central America with her children, became after a sought after copy writer and started a course: 30 Days To Social Media Sales.
It was during her healing journey that she also started offering community meals & opened a community center where she would ask people what their dreams were... and then help those become true!
From that foundation Courageous Dreamers was birthed with the mission to serve and create a community of Courageous Dreamers, Entrepreneurs, Leaders, Visionaries, and Creatives who are ready to become the most joyful, inspirational, and prosperous versions of themselves while manifesting their individual & company dreams into reality as well as helping others realize their dreams as well.
