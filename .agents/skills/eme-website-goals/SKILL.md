# EME web site goals

Goals set by the user on 2026-11-25. These are the product plans: eME Salesbot, eME Team and eME Pro are
what we want to sell. Do not assume any of them is built yet. Check the code before saying a feature works.

## Goals of the site

1. Sell **eME Salesbot**, an AI chatbot for businesses.
2. Show that Salesbot comes with **eME Team**, the app employees use to do the follow up work.
3. Sell **eME Pro** to businesses that need something custom built.

## Who sells

EME World Inc. sells everything directly: eME Salesbot, eME Pro, consulting and hosting. It is a company, no longer a

### eME Salesbot: what customers can do

- Ask sales questions.
- Get support.
- Schedule a meeting.
- Place orders from a product menu.

### eME Team: what employees can do

- Respond to open tickets.
- Complete goals.
- Do bookkeeping: profit and loss (P&L), income and expenses.
- Use basic CRM features.
- Read technical support documentation.

### eME Pro

A custom AI server and employee app, built by AI to the needs of one business.

## How the pieces connect

A customer chats with eME Salesbot. What needs a person (a question, a ticket, a meeting, an order) goes to
employees in eME Team. A business that outgrows the standard product moves to eME Pro.

Tell it in that order: customer, then team, then custom.

## The principle behind every product

eME augments the staff a business already has. It does not create synthetic agents to replace them. Each employee trains an
electronic version of themselves to be more productive and to carry less cognitive burden.

- **eME Salesbot** handles first contact and passes real work to real people in eME Team. Present it as the front desk for your
  team, not a replacement for it.
- **eME Team** is where each employee's own eME (eMike for Mike, eChristopher for Christopher) helps them close tickets, track goals, keep the books and find answers.
- **eME Pro** builds the server around how your people already work.

## Writing about the products

- Use the names exactly: eME Salesbot, eME Team, eME Pro. eME means electronic me, spelled like email. Rules are in `eme-brand-voice`.
- Lead with what the customer or employee can do, then the price.
- Show prices plainly. Always give both eME Pro numbers together.
- Humans stay in control: the bot answers and collects, people decide.
- Sell lower cognitive burden and higher productivity for the people you have, never fewer people.
- Copy rules are in `eme-brand-voice`, look and page conventions in `eme-brand-site`, sales approach in
  `eme-sales-marketing`.
- Do not invent features, limits, discounts, free trials, contract terms or customers.
