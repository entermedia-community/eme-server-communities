## Positioning: on the side of the many, not the few (set 2026-11-25)

This replaces the older "Collective Intelligence Platform" framing. The story is now the conflict between two kinds of AI:

| Closed and commercial (the few) | Open and collective (the many) |
|---|---|
| Closed models and closed source | Open models and open source |
| Commercial control by a few companies | Distributed, run by the people who use it |
| Centralized knowledge stores that hold what everyone creates | What you create stays yours and adds to the shared open source ecosystem |
| The population depends on, and is steered by, whoever owns the store | Communities and businesses keep control of their own knowledge |

- **What makes us different (repeat it often):** eME is the open source, open model, self hosted AI platform that does not depend on big tech providers. Say all four parts together: open source, open models, self hosted, independent of big tech providers. Do not claim to be "the only" one; other open source projects exist. This is about providers, not customers: we want big customers (the United Nations, Yale and CBC are proof points), we just do not run on big tech models, clouds or APIs. Never word it as being against large organizations or corporations.
- **Full AI API with flat pricing:** eME offers a complete AI API (OpenAI syntax compatible) at a flat price, with no caps on token usage. Use it as a headline offer, not a footnote. Wording: "A full AI API. Flat pricing. No caps on tokens."
- **Name for what we make:** **collective intelligence technologies**. Keep the words "collective intelligence", but as the technologies on the open side of this conflict, not as a product category.
- **Stance line:** "On the side of the many, not the few."
- **Proof of the stance:** 100% open source stack, open models on dedicated hardware, no data harvesting, no vendor lock in, every server and skill built adds to the open ecosystem.
- **Tone:** clear about the stakes, never angry. Name the model (closed, centralized, commercial control), not specific companies. The few means closed big tech providers, never big customers.
- Older lines below still work where they fit, but lead with this contrast.

- **Older H1:** "Collective Intelligence Platform". Retire it as the main headline; use "infrastructure" only as a supporting word for government and enterprise copy. Tagline: "Empowering Collective Intelligence".
- **Eyebrow / vision line:** "Customizable AI for Everyone".
- **Sub line:** "One open platform. Every AI need. Humans fully in control."
- **Lead:** "EME is an all inclusive AI package delivered through a single, open platform, fully customizable using AI skills."
- **Sign off:** "Own your intelligence. Own your content. Own your future."

## The one idea

AI should serve the many, not the few. Closed, commercial AI gathers everyone's knowledge into a few centralized stores. eME is
distributed and open source, so everything we create joins the collective open source ecosystem. eME gives organizations one open, sovereign place for every AI need,
and **humans stay in control**. The assistant never acts on its own for key actions; it relies on human guidance.

**Augment staff, do not replace them.** eME does not create synthetic agents that stand in for workers. It augments the staff
you already have. Each person trains an electronic version of themselves (their eME, named eChristopher, eMike and so on) to raise their productivity and lower their
cognitive burden. The goal is never fewer people; it is each person carrying less and getting more done.

"Agents" stays as a technical capability (harness cards, the Agent entity, the AI economy over chat or MCP). Present agents as
tools that people direct, never as synthetic staff.

## Core ideas (use these exact names)

| Name | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **eME (electronic me)** | Spelled eME, like email: the e stands for electronic. The premise of the product: each of us will have an electronic version of ourselves. A person's eME is their AI assistant, trained as a personal **digital twin** (their style, schedule, knowledge). The entity is called an **eME Profile**. Do not call it a "persona". | **EME Server** | A custom server a team builds on a 100% open source stack, reached through simple chat screens with forms, data tables and graphics. |
| **Chat as the front door** | Everything starts in a conversation. |
| **Open API** | A full AI API offering: complete REST API, OpenAI syntax compatible, flat pricing, no caps on token usage and no usage charges. Position it as a main offer. |
| **Deployment model** | Predictable flat rate, full data privacy, no corporate connections, no data harvesting, no vendor lock in. Includes hosting, open AI models, dedicated GPU and cloud storage. **Unlimited tokens** on **dedicated hardware**, with no usage charges. |

## What the user prioritized (shapes every new piece)

- **Most compelling parts only, not too many words.** Lead with the mission, the trust proof (UN, Yale, CBC), free for everyone, unlimited tokens on dedicated hardware, and the digital brain and AI economy. Cut paragraphs to one line.
- **Two levels of detail.** Pictures show the essentials (standard harness components, the entity ring). Cards carry everything else, with descriptive text.
- **Practical features matter:** Docker, Git and Linux friendly with deploy direct from Git, Server Sync, Desktop File Manager, MCP server and client, Scheduler, Job Manager, vector search, embeddings, entity recognition, automated tagging, facial recognition, Markdown creation, image creation, and the visual tools (Smart Organizer, Workflow Editor, Smart Creator).
- **Terminology is part of the brand:** the entities are Person, EME Profile, Team, Project, Server, Channel, Document, Asset, File, Category, Collection, Skill, Agent.

## Never claim

- That the AI acts autonomously or replaces people.
- That eME provides AI employees, digital workers or synthetic agents, or that it reduces headcount.
- Usage based pricing, vendor lock in, or data sharing with corporations.
- Customers, numbers or integrations that are not in the source documents.
