## The one idea

AI should serve people and communities, not corporations. EME gives organizations one open, sovereign place for every AI need,
and **humans stay in control**. The assistant never acts on its own for key actions; it relies on human guidance.

## Core ideas (use these exact names)

| Name | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **EME (Electronic Me)** | A person's AI assistant persona, trained as a personal **digital twin** (their style, schedule, knowledge). |
| **People Driven** | Users add reference files, team guidelines and preferences. Two example modes: *Clear and Precise*, *Friendly and Helpful*. |
| **Omnichannel Communication** | My EME connects app users with teammates who use email. |
| **AI Harness** | The central control system and safety framework. Standardizes how AI tools run tasks, protect security and interact across the organization. |
| **EME Server** | A custom server a team builds on a 100% open source stack, reached through simple chat screens with forms, data tables and graphics. |
| **Chat as the front door** | Everything starts in a conversation. |
| **Open API** | Complete REST API, OpenAI syntax compatible, available to EME servers with no extra charge for usage. |
| **Deployment model** | Predictable flat rate, full data privacy, no corporate connections, no data harvesting, no vendor lock in. Includes hosting, open AI models, dedicated GPU and cloud storage. |

The 13 harness features, in order: Memory, Agents, Chat, Automation, Informatics, Database, Media Management, Storage,
Translation, Transcribing & Voice to Text, Video Editing, Orchestration, Open API.

How knowledge is structured (diagram "How to structure organizational knowledge"): **People** (individual level, My EME),
**Projects** (collaborative team workspaces), **Servers** (flexible, self hosted platform).

## Proof points (real examples, do not invent others)

- **UN News EME:** editorial workflow from ingest, transcription and indexing, draft synthesis, multilingual and alt text, editorial review, push to Drupal CMS, publication.
- **Cacao Source:** mobile team chat with Employee Training, Company Dashboard and Goal Tracking.
- **Cumbre de Cocreación:** Business Coach, Capacitación y Certificación (LevelUp Guatemala courses), Directorio de Negocios.
- **Example server apps:** Content Creation, Employee Training, Community Bank, Business Coaching, Workforce Scheduling, Regional Ride-Share.
- **Scenario A:** Christopher and Tammy plan a weekend event; EME Christopher checks calendars and offers tickets and a carpool inside the chat.

## Audience

Developers get the tutorial page (`developers.html`): setup from GitHub, OpenCode to customize the server.

- Usage based pricing, vendor lock in, or data sharing with corporations.
- Customers, numbers or integrations that are not in the source documents.
